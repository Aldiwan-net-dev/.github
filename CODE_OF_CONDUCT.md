# Code of conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone. Be respectful, assume good intent, discuss ideas rather than people, and give actionable feedback. Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, and publishing private information are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Report conduct concerns privately through the contact listed on [developers.aldiwan.net](https://developers.aldiwan.net).
